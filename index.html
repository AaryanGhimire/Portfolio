About

I'm a BE IT student in Nepal, currently through my sixth semester. Most of what I know, I've learned by building — starting with small, rough projects and working toward systems with more moving parts.

My interest sits closer to the backend than the interface: databases, data flow, the logic that keeps something running when nobody's watching it work. Python is where I spend most of my time outside coursework, usually pointed at automation, small AI/ML experiments, or IoT ideas — projects built to understand how something works, not to ship a product.

Robotics and automation interest me for the same reason: software that has to deal with a messier, physical world instead of a clean API. I'm still early — I haven't worked professionally yet — but I take the fundamentals seriously and I'd rather understand a system properly than fake my way past it.
